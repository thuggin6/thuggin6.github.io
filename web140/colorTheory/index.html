Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I chose these colors because they allow for a balance. The colors don't overwhelm the page but rather draws you in to View the page.

Mood: This palette creates a calming relaxing mood. Sets the page to be warm and balanced.

Website Usage: I would use the primary color for Header and major section. The background color allow for the sections to stand out. The accent colors can be used for buttons. The accent color draws the user's eyes to the button. The secondary colors can be used for headers and paragraphs.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: I selected these colors because of their warm tones. The color palette gives a natural feeling of autumn leaves.

Mood: This palette gives a feeling of comfort and serenity.

Website Usage: EI would use the primary color for Header and major sections. The background color allows for the sections to stand out. The accent colors can be used for buttons. I would switch out the accent and secondary colors when using the div tags, headers, and paragraphs to give a better contrast.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I chose these colors because of their contrast and projects energy.

Mood: These are eyecatching colors that demand attention. The colors gives the feeling of energy.
